How to play

Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 grid. Fill a complete row or column and it clears. Clear on consecutive placements to build a combo multiplier. The run ends when none of the pieces in your tray will fit anywhere.

Every tray you are dealt is checked in advance to make sure it can actually be played, so a run never ends because the game handed you a piece with nowhere to go.

Common questions

My score disappeared. Scores are stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes them, and they do not sync between devices.

Where did my game in progress go? A run in progress is saved when you leave the app and offered back as "Continue" on the home screen. Starting a new game in the same mode discards it.

The Daily Challenge board changed. The daily board is tied to the UTC date and resets at midnight UTC, which may not be midnight where you are.

I don't see a leaderboard. Leaderboards require being signed in to Game Center. If you have declined Game Center, the button does not appear.

How do I turn off vibration? Tap the pause button during a game, or use the toggle at the bottom of the home screen.
