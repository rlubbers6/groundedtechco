Privacy Policy

Gridlock: Block Puzzle · Last updated 5 September 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Gridlock is a puzzle game for iPhone, published by Ryan Lubbers. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. It applies to the Gridlock iOS app and to this website.

The short version

Gridlock has no accounts and no sign-in. Your scores and your game in progress are stored on your device and are never sent anywhere. The app is free and supported by advertising, and the advertising provider collects some data in order to serve ads.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone and is not transmitted:

  • Your best scores for Endless and Daily Challenge
  • A game in progress, so it can be resumed
  • Your vibration preference and advertising consent choices

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising

Gridlock shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • Approximate (coarse) location, derived from your IP address
  • Information about your interaction with ads, such as views and taps
  • Device and diagnostic information, such as device model and operating system version

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. Google's disclosure of how it uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Gridlock does not collect this data itself, does not have access to it in an identifiable form, and does not sell it.

Your choices about tracking

App Tracking Transparency. On first launch, iOS asks whether Gridlock may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the IDFA is not used and you will see less relevant ads. The game itself is unaffected either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, Gridlock presents a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before any ads are requested. You can decline personalised advertising there, and your choice is respected on subsequent launches.

Limiting ad tracking system-wide. iOS provides Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising to control personalised advertising across apps.

Game Center

Leaderboards are optional and provided by Apple's Game Center. If you sign in, your Game Center player alias and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Gridlock submits only a numeric score. Apple's handling of Game Center data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. Declining Game Center hides the leaderboard and changes nothing else.

Analytics

Gridlock includes no separate analytics service. No crash reporting, behavioural analytics, or attribution SDK is present beyond what Google AdMob performs to serve and measure advertising.

Children

Gridlock is rated 4+ but is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It is not distributed in the App Store Kids Category. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the address below and it will be removed.

Data retention and transfers

Because Gridlock stores nothing on its own servers — it has none — there is no data for the developer to retain or delete. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own policies and may be processed outside your country.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to object to certain processing. Since the developer holds no personal data about you, such requests are directed to Google for advertising data. You can review and control the data Google associates with you at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is collected will be noted in the app's release notes.
